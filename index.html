About Me

Why AI, why now

I've always loved AI and the similarity it has with humans — but what fascinates me just as much is how it's actually built, and the sheer range of solutions it can bring to real problems. That fascination is what pulled me into learning Claude and AI development hands-on: going from curious observer to someone who builds, tests, and ships with these tools every day.

I'm not coming at this from a traditional CS background — I'm learning by doing: writing real prompts, shipping small Python tools, wiring up data pipelines, and documenting what breaks along the way so the next build goes faster. Every project on this page is something I actually built, not a tutorial I copied.

Right now my focus is turning AI and data skills into things that solve real problems — for people, for businesses, for messy real-world datasets — and getting better, one project at a time.
